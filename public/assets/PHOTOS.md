## Named crops (`scripts/make-images.js`, `CROPS`)

Each crop is a window cut from a source at a fixed aspect, centred on a focal point, then resized. `npm run images` writes them to `public/assets/derived/<name>-<width>.<webp|jpg>` and records the window in `manifest.json` under `original.crop`.

| Crop | Source, window | Shows | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `hero-clay-32` | clay photo, 3:2, full width, centred on both players' faces | Katie (left) and Eva (right) from the racket to the net | Homepage hero from 700px; the share images (`/assets/og/*.jpg`) |
| `hero-clay-45` | clay photo, 4:5, full width | Both players, head to foot | Homepage hero on phones (preloaded) |
| `katie-portrait-34` | clay photo, 3:4, the left 52% | Katie alone, racket raised | Katie's profile portrait on `/pros`, the `/pros` intro link, the homepage teaser |
| `eva-portrait-34` | clay photo, 3:4, the right 42% | Eva alone, white visor | Eva's profile portrait on `/pros`, the `/pros` intro link, the homepage teaser |
| `spain-card-43` | clay photo, 4:3, full width, faces and the net | Both players at the net | The featured Spain card on `/` and the Spain trip card on `/pros` |
| `coast-small-11` | coast selfie, 1:1, centred | Katie and Eva on the coast | Small, in the "A pro hosts it" column on `/` |

Derived files live in `public/assets/derived/` and are generated by `npm run images`. Crops are declared in `scripts/make-images.js` with a named focal point; never crop by hand in CSS alone when a derived crop exists (the old `.crop-right` 200%-width trick is gone). EXIF metadata was stripped from the originals on 2026-10-05; run `scripts/strip-exif.js` on any new photo before adding it. The share images and the touch icon come from `node scripts/make-og.js` after `npm run images`.
