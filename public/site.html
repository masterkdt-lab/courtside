One way to spend it

The Sunday–Saturday
rhythm.

Sunday is arrival. Tennis and curated experiences run Monday through Friday. Saturday is departure. We plan enough to make it effortless, then deliberately leave space.

Sunday

Arrive + settle in

Airport pickup, La Finca check-in, unpack, then a relaxed welcome dinner with Katie.

Monday

Tennis + resort afternoon

Breakfast, tennis with Katie + Eva, long lunch, then spa, golf, gym or nothing.

Tuesday

Clay + the coast

Morning tennis followed by La Mata / Torrevieja, a coastal lunch and a relaxed evening.

Wednesday

Do less.

No mandatory tennis. Sleep in, golf, book a massage, work, read, or opt into a casual hit.

Thursday

Katie + Eva + a day out

Morning tennis with Katie + Eva, then Alicante, Cartagena, shopping, food or another curated outing.

Friday

The finale

Match play, recovery, and one great final dinner. The exact rhythm can flex with the group.

Saturday

Breakfast + departure

Checkout and private airport transfer around the flights you booked independently.
