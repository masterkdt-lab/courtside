How it works

From invitation to court.

Courtside runs on invitations, not bookings. This is how a week comes together.

  1. 1

    Get your invitation.

    Katie invites her community first. Have one already? Enter it here

  2. 2

    Choose your week, or let Katie curate your group.

    Bring 4–12 players for a private week, or come solo or as a couple and Katie builds the group around fit.

  3. 3

    Katie confirms the details with you.

    Rooms, court time and the rhythm of the week are confirmed personally. Then you book your flights and come play.
