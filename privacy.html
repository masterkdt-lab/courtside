keeps, and why.

The Spanish Offseason site is invitation-only. This page says, in plain words, what the site stores when you use it.

Your invitation code

When you enter your code, we check it against Katie’s guest list. We keep a note that the code was used, and when, so invitations can be managed, and we log each attempt to keep the site private. Your browser keeps a small cookie so you don’t have to enter the code again for 30 days.

The request you send

When you send a request from the builder, we store what you chose: the week or weeks, your group, stay and tennis preferences, add-ons, your notes and the working estimate shown on screen, together with the name, email and mobile number on your invitation. Katie uses this to review your request and get back to you.

The email we send you

When your invitation includes an email address, we send you one confirmation email with your request ID and a copy of your brief. Katie receives a copy of every request.

Nothing else

There are no accounts and no payments on this site. Questions about any of this? Reply to your invitation from Katie.
